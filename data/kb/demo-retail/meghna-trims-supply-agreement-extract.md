# Meghna Trims supply agreement (extract)

> Synthetic demo document. "Meghna Trims Ltd", "Demo Retail" and every clause, name and figure
> below are fictional and exist only to demonstrate Keelson. This is not a real contract and not
> legal advice.

Parties: **Demo Retail B.V.** ("the Buyer") and **Meghna Trims Ltd**, supplier code TRIM-BD-01
("the Supplier"). The Supplier delivers trims (wadding CMP-WAD-01 and zips CMP-ZIP-01) to the
Buyer's nominated garment factories F-BD-01 (Gazipur) and F-BD-02 (Narayanganj).

## 4. Delivery and lead times

4.1 The Supplier delivers trims to the nominated factory no later than the "trim-in date" on the
purchase order. The standard lead time is 21 days from order confirmation.

4.2 The Supplier keeps a safety stock of at least two weeks of forecast consumption of wadding and
zips for the Buyer's peak season orders (ex-factory weeks 36 to 44).

## 7. Notice of delay

7.1 The Supplier must notify the Buyer **in writing within 48 hours** of becoming aware of any
event that is likely to delay a trim-in date by more than 3 days.

7.2 The notice must state the affected purchase orders, the cause, the expected new trim-in date
and the mitigation the Supplier proposes.

7.3 Messages sent through the supplier portal count as written notice. A notice that does not
state an expected new date is incomplete; the Buyer may then assume a delay of 14 days for
planning purposes until a complete notice arrives.

7.4 The Supplier must update the notice at least every 72 hours until the delay is resolved.

## 9. Late delivery

9.1 For each full day of delay beyond the trim-in date that is not excused under clause 12, the
Supplier pays liquidated damages of 0.5% of the value of the delayed trims, capped at 10% of
that value.

9.2 If a delay that is not excused exceeds 10 days, the Buyer may source the affected trims or the
finished garments from an alternative approved supplier and recover the reasonable extra cost
(including expedited freight) from the Supplier, up to the cap in clause 9.3.

9.3 The Supplier's total liability under this clause is capped at the value of the affected
purchase orders.

## 12. Force majeure

12.1 Neither party is liable for a delay caused by an event beyond its reasonable control,
including flood, cyclone, fire, epidemic, port closure, national strikes and government action
("force majeure"), **provided the affected party gives notice under clause 12.2**.

12.2 The affected party must give written notice of a force majeure event **within 5 working days**
of its start, with evidence (for example an official weather warning or a port authority notice).
Without timely notice the event does not excuse the delay.

12.3 A force majeure event does not excuse a delay that the Supplier could have avoided by holding
the safety stock required by clause 4.2.

12.4 If a force majeure event lasts more than **30 days**, either party may cancel the affected
purchase orders without liability. The Buyer may in any case place replacement orders with
alternative approved suppliers during a force majeure event, and those orders do not breach any
exclusivity terms.

## 15. Communication

15.1 Operational communication goes through the Buyer's supplier portal. The Buyer's sourcing desk
answers within one working day.

15.2 Nothing in a supplier message changes this agreement. Changes to quantities, prices or dates
are only valid when confirmed by a purchase order revision issued by the Buyer.
