# Freight & expedite policy (sea-air premium caps)

> Synthetic demo document. "Demo Retail" and every lane, rate, cap and figure in this document are
> fictional and exist only to demonstrate Keelson. Not real freight guidance.

## 1. Default modes

- Asian sourcing ships by **sea** to Felixstowe, Rotterdam, Hamburg or Gdańsk. Default routing is
  the one in the carrier contract for the season (via the Suez Canal or around the Cape of Good
  Hope).
- Near-shore sourcing from Türkiye and Morocco ships by **road** (Istanbul) or **short-sea plus
  road** (Tanger Med).
- DC-to-DC transfers go by road (see the DC-to-DC transfer SOP).

## 2. When expediting is allowed

Expedited freight (sea-air or air) may be used only when all of these are true:

1. A shortage is forecast in a peak week (W44 to W52) for an A-grade cluster or for online orders
   with a promise date.
2. Rebalancing between DCs cannot close the gap in time.
3. The expedited units arrive at least 3 days before the first week of shortage.

## 3. Sea-air via Jebel Ali

The preferred expedite mode is **sea-air through Jebel Ali (AEJEA)**: sea leg from the origin port
to Jebel Ali, then air freight to the European DC. Transit is typically 8 to 12 days door to DC,
against 30 to 40 days by sea.

- Book sea-air capacity at least 5 days before the vessel's cut-off.
- Weekly capacity on the contracted sea-air lane is limited; the planner must check the remaining
  capacity for the week before proposing the split.
- Only split the sizes and colours that are actually short. Do not move whole purchase orders.

## 4. Premium caps

| Mode | Premium cap per unit | Cap per case | Approver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sea-air via Jebel Ali | 2.5x the sea cost per unit | EUR 25,000 | Head of Supply |
| Air (direct) | 6x the sea cost per unit | EUR 15,000 | Head of Supply and Finance Business Partner |
| Courier / hand-carry | Not allowed for stock replenishment | n/a | n/a |

- The cap per case is the total extra freight cost of one disruption case, across all lines.
- A proposal above a cap needs written approval from the Supply Director in addition to the normal
  approvers.
- Expedite premiums caused by a supplier delay that is not excused are recovered from the supplier
  where the supply agreement allows it (for example clause 9.2 of the Meghna Trims agreement).

## 5. Air is the last resort

Direct air freight is only used when sea-air cannot arrive in time, and never for bulky outerwear
when sea-air is available in the same week.

## 6. Records

Every expedite booking records: the case it belongs to, the purchase order lines and quantities
moved, the lane, the expected arrival and the premium paid. Finance reconciles premiums monthly.
